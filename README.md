# Seminar Presentation Website: "Trapped by Design"

A modern, cinematic web presentation built for your seminar. It runs directly in any browser without needing to install anything.

---

## 🚀 How to Open the Presentation

1. Open your File Explorer and go to your folder:
   `C:\Users\Support\Desktop\Seminar`
2. Double-click **`index.html`**.
3. It will immediately open in your default web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, etc.).

---

## 🎮 How to Navigate the Slides

You have multiple easy ways to move forward and backward:

| Action | What to Press or Click |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Next / Forward Slide** | • Press **`Right Arrow →`** on your keyboard<br>• Press **`Spacebar`** or **`PageDown`**<br>• Click the **"Next"** button on the bottom bar<br>• Swipe left on touch screens |
| **Previous / Backward Slide** | • Press **`Left Arrow ←`** on your keyboard<br>• Press **`Backspace`** or **`PageUp`**<br>• Click the **"Back"** button on the bottom bar<br>• Swipe right on touch screens |
| **Jump Directly to a Slide** | • Click any of the **dots** on the bottom navigation bar<br>• Press **`1`** for Slide 1 or **`2`** for Slide 2 on your keyboard |
| **Speaker Script / Notes** | • Press **`S`** on your keyboard<br>• Or click the **"Speaker Script"** button at the top right |
| **Fullscreen Mode** | • Press **`F`** on your keyboard<br>• Or click the fullscreen icon at the top right |
| **Seminar Timer** | • Automatically starts counting elapsed time in the top bar (`00:00`)<br>• Click the timer or press **`T`** to pause or resume |
| **Keyboard Shortcuts Help** | • Click the **`?`** icon in the top right |

---

## 🎤 Speaker Script & Presenter Features

When you press **`S`** (or click **Speaker Script**), a smooth presenter drawer appears at the bottom:
- Displays your exact spoken script in large, crisp text.
- Includes **A-** and **A+** buttons to adjust text size so you can read comfortably while presenting.
- Displays an **estimated speaking time** and **word count** for each slide.
- Does not interrupt the slide animations or audience visuals.
